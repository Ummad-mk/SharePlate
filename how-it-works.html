How SharePlate Rescues Food

Our hyper-local digital pipeline ensures surplus meals go from commercial kitchens and homes directly to hungry bellies in under 60 minutes.

🍲

1. Donors Post Instantly

When banquets or restaurants have leftover untouched food, staff take 30 seconds to snap a photo, enter approximate portions, and log the time prepared.

  • ✓ Thermal safety verification
  • ✓ Wedding pre-schedule toggle
  • ✓ Automatic nearest hub broadcast
📱

2. Receivers Claim

Registered shelters, orphanages, and community food pantries receive real-time push alerts. They review portion size and claim what matches their capacity.

  • ✓ Secure 4-digit verification code
  • ✓ Fair distribution algorithm
  • ✓ Zero cost to verified beneficiaries
🛵

3. Volunteers Deliver

Vetted local volunteers accept rescue tasks nearby, pick up food in temperature-controlled transport bags, and complete contactless handover.

  • ✓ Live GPS navigation
  • ✓ Digital signature & photo proof
  • ✓ Community karma & badges
Quality First

Strict Food Safety Standards

Food dignity means serving meals you would gladly feed your own family.

🌡️ The Two-Hour Rule

Cooked food must be kept above 60°C (hot hold) or cooled below 5°C. Hot food must be consumed or refrigerated within 2 hours of leaving thermal control.

📦 Hygiene Packaging Checklist

All surplus must be placed in clean, food-grade airtight containers or sealed foil catering trays. Unsealed or partially eaten plate waste is strictly prohibited.

🛡️ Good Samaritan Protection

Donors acting in good faith who comply with hygienic standard procedures are legally shielded under national surplus food donation guidelines.

Safety Verification Protocol

✓
Smell, Visual & Texture Check: Confirmed fresh before listing.
✓
Allergen Labeling: Nuts, dairy, gluten, and non-veg labeled distinctly.
✓
Volunteer Sanitization: Thermal delivery kits cleaned after every drop.
✓
Receiver Confirmation: Temperature check recorded on handover.
Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any charge for NGOs or shelters?

No. SharePlate is 100% free for charitable receivers. All food is donated freely, and volunteer rescues are powered by community goodwill.

How does wedding mode work?

Wedding and event hosts can pre-schedule a surplus window days ahead. Our dispatcher reserves dedicated volunteer teams to arrive at 11:30 PM with clean insulated barrels.

Can individual households donate single meals?

Yes! Even 2 to 4 portions of clean home-cooked food can be picked up by neighborhood volunteers walking or cycling nearby.
