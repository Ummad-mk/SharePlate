How SharePlate Works

Food rescues take under 45 minutes on average, preventing edible meals from reaching landfills.

📱

1. Post Surplus

Donors snap a photo, enter quantity, and set safe consumption time. Wedding mode enables scheduled large-batch pickups.

🤝

2. Instant Match

Nearby verified orphanages, shelters, and hunger relief NGOs get notified instantly and claim the exact food quantity they need.

🚴

3. Swift Delivery

Community volunteers on two-wheelers pick up with insulated thermal bags and deliver safely with live GPS status tracking.

Stories of Hope

Real moments of dignity, kindness, and nourishment across our network.

General Manager, Nishat Banquet Complex, Gulberg Lahore
